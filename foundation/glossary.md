Every community grows its own language. This page defines the words this handbook uses, so you don't need to have been around since day one to follow along. Each entry links to where the term lives, so you can read the full context.

If a word is missing or a definition doesn't match how people actually use it, say so. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Who We Are

**Builder** — Anyone who makes things: code, designs, tools, systems, experiences, events. Here it's a posture, not a job title. It means seeing a problem and starting to work on it instead of waiting for permission. See [Builder Philosophy](../philosophy/builder-philosophy.md).

**By us, for us** — PhilaCon Valley is built by the people it serves: Black, Brown, LGBTQIA+, and underrepresented technologists in Philadelphia. They aren't a niche audience here. They're the audience. See [Mission](mission.md).

**Code-switching** — Changing how you talk, dress, or carry yourself so you fit in with the dominant culture of a room. "No code-switching" means you don't have to perform a different version of yourself to belong here. See [Principles](principles.md).

**Curiosity over credentials** — You don't need a degree, a title, or years of experience to belong. Asking questions and learning count for more than a résumé. See [Principles](principles.md).

**Gatekeeping** — Deciding who gets in based on credentials, insider knowledge, or "paying your dues." We don't do it. Mentoring is the opposite of gatekeeping, and it's what we do instead. See [Principles](principles.md) and [What We Believe](vision.md).

**Philly first** — We're rooted in Philadelphia, and our city's stories, culture, and people are part of what we build. We aren't trying to copy Silicon Valley. See [Principles](principles.md).

**Tech-curious** — Someone who is interested in tech but isn't (or isn't yet) working in it. You're welcome here as much as a senior engineer. See [README](../README.md).

## Events

**Build night** — An evening event where people spend the whole time making something together, not watching talks. PATCH is our build-night series.

**PATCH** — Our flagship build night. One theme, one night, hands-on the whole time. People from different crafts (sound, visuals, code) "patch" their work into one build. Running since June 2026 and numbered (PATCH 001, PATCH 002, and counting). See [Event Philosophy](../philosophy/event-philosophy.md).

**R&D lab** — The space where the nonprofit tests new ideas before they're proven. Some of what works here later becomes paid work through the agency arm. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#how-philacon-valley-is-organized).

**Workshops, Vision Labs, Hackathons, The Giveback** — The other event formats in our lineup: focused skill-building, big-picture discussion, longer build sprints, and events that give value back to the community. We're building toward these. They aren't running regularly yet, and [Event Philosophy](../philosophy/event-philosophy.md) will say so when each one does.

## How We're Organized

**Agency arm** — PhilaCon Valley, LLC. It packages proven community work into paid projects for outside clients, and that revenue helps fund the community. See [Builder Philosophy](../philosophy/builder-philosophy.md#building-happens-on-two-tracks--and-both-count).

**Area of focus (zone)** — A part of the work that one team member owns and drives, like product design or brand. Zones keep things from falling through the cracks. They aren't silos, and everyone pitches in across them. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#what-the-team-does).

**Board** — The governing board PhilaCon Valley's nonprofit arm is moving toward. Conversations are underway, but nobody is seated yet. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#moving-toward-a-board).

**Contracted specialist** — A team member who owns a defined area of the work under a paid contractor agreement. Diego Mendoza (Product Design Lead) and Saige Sufrin (Brand Designer & Creative Director) are contracted specialists. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#the-team).

**Founder and Executive Director** — The person with overall responsibility for PhilaCon Valley's direction and day-to-day operations, and the one who makes the final call on community-wide decisions. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#the-team).

**Nonprofit arm** — PhilaCon Valley, Inc. (formation in progress). It runs the community programs: build nights, workshops, hackathons, and the R&D lab. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#how-philacon-valley-is-organized).

**Organizer** — In this handbook, the Founder and Executive Director's community-leadership role specifically. Contracted specialists hold contractor titles instead. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#the-team).

**Stewardship** — Taking care of the community instead of controlling it. "Community-stewarded" means governance stays lightweight, transparent, and open to change, and that a title is about service, not power. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md).

**Team / team member** — Everyone who helps run PhilaCon Valley in a defined role: the Founder and Executive Director plus the contracted specialists. When the handbook says "the team," this is who it means. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#the-team).

**Two tracks** — The two kinds of work builders do here: community work through the nonprofit ("what do we want to make?") and client work through the agency ("what does this client need?"). Both count, and both are held to the same standard. See [Builder Philosophy](../philosophy/builder-philosophy.md#building-happens-on-two-tracks--and-both-count).

**Workgroup** — A temporary group formed for one initiative, like a hackathon series or a mentorship program. It has its own lead and timeline, and it dissolves when the work is done. This is a structure we expect to use as we grow, not one that exists today. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#where-were-heading).

## How Decisions Get Made

**Day-to-day decision** — An operational call inside one team member's zone, like scheduling or a design choice. The person who owns the zone decides as things come up, without polling the room. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#day-to-day-decisions).

**Listen, then lead** — Our decision-making model. The community's input shapes a decision, and the Founder and Executive Director owns the final call. If the decision goes against what the community said, they explain why. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#the-principle-behind-it).

**Propose → Listen → Decide → Communicate** — The four steps for significant decisions: someone puts an idea forward, the community weighs in, a decision is made, and the decision is shared along with the reasoning. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#significant-decisions).

**Recusal** — Stepping out of a decision because you're involved in it. If a conduct report is about a team member, that person has no part in handling it. See [Community Guidelines](../governance/community-guidelines.md#enforcement).

**Significant decision** — A change that affects the community's direction, culture, or structure, like a new event format, a partnership, or a meaningful handbook update. These go through Propose → Listen → Decide → Communicate. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#significant-decisions).

## How We Treat Each Other

**Bad-faith participation** — Showing up mainly to recruit, sell, promote, or take value from the community without contributing anything. See [Community Guidelines](../governance/community-guidelines.md#unacceptable-behavior).

**Follow before you lead** — What we ask of allies. You're welcome here, but this community's priorities are clear, so listen and learn before you try to steer. See [Community Guidelines](../governance/community-guidelines.md#expected-behavior).

**Make space, take space** — If you usually talk a lot, leave room for others. If you usually stay quiet, know that your voice matters and we want to hear it. See [Community Guidelines](../governance/community-guidelines.md#expected-behavior).

## AI and Our Work

**Agentic engineering** — The AI skill this community builds around: using AI as a working partner inside a real build, not as a one-off trick. A "vibe coding" session was a single workshop format we tried once. It isn't how we build here. See [AI Principles](../philosophy/ai-principles.md).

**Hand-drawn marks** — The mascot, the wordmark, and the lettering they're built from. They're drawn by hand and never get AI-generated, traced, or rebuilt. Resizing an official file or removing its background is fine. See [AI Principles](../philosophy/ai-principles.md#what-we-ask-of-everyone-else).

## About This Handbook

**Draft / Active** — The two statuses a handbook document can have. Draft means it's still open for input and expected to change. Active means it reflects current practice, and changes to it should be well considered. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#document-status).

**Four-layer documentation model** — Where PhilaCon Valley information lives. GitHub (this handbook) holds what we believe. Discord is where conversation happens. Google Drive holds internal working documents. The website holds public storytelling. See [README](../README.md#where-documentation-lives).

**Last Reviewed** — The date at the top of each document showing when someone last checked it against how things actually are. It isn't the date of the last edit. The handbook gets a fact-check every quarter. See [Governance](../governance/governance.md#keeping-the-handbook-current).

**Source of truth** — The place that wins when two sources disagree. For philosophy, values, principles, and governance, that's this handbook.
